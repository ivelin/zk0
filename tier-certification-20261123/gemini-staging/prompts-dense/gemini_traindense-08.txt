# Judging brief — training-demo certification audit (2026-11-23)

You are judging whether recorded human-teleoperated demonstration episodes
actually completed their task. Each episode has ONE contact-sheet PNG:
two rows (FRONT camera, TOP camera), six frames each, left→right in time
(0%, 35%, 60%, 80%, 92%, ~100% of the episode). Judge the END STATE —
what the final frames show — not how elegant the motion was.

## Verdicts (exactly one per episode)

- PASS — the final frames clearly show the task's completion end-state
  (definitions below), with the gripper no longer holding the object.
- FAIL — the final frames clearly show the end-state is NOT the
  completion state (object never placed, placed wrong, dropped, stack
  not standing, etc.). A success achieved mid-episode but undone by the
  end is FAIL — end state only.
- INDETERMINATE — the final frames do not let you determine the end
  state (object occluded in both views at the end, ambiguous seating,
  unreadable frames). Use sparingly; if one camera shows it clearly,
  judge from that.

## Per-task completion definitions (Study 001 frozen criteria, 2026-11-20)

- block_stack ("Stack the colouful blocks on top of each other"):
  every block in the scene is part of one vertical stack — each block
  resting on another block or on the base block — gripper released,
  stack standing at episode end.
- eye_drops_to_basket ("Put the eye drops into the basket"): the
  eye-drops bottle is inside the basket — resting within the basket's
  walls, no longer held by the gripper — at episode end.
- ring_insert ("Insert the colourful ring into the central wooden
  peg"): the ring encircles the shaft of the central wooden peg —
  threaded onto the peg, released by the gripper — at episode end. A
  ring resting on top of the peg's tip without encircling the shaft is
  NOT a completion. A ring on any other peg is FAIL.
- tool_insert ("Insert the missing tool into the empty slot on the
  toolbox"): the tool that was missing from the toolbox at episode
  start is inserted into the empty slot, seated in its place, and
  released by the gripper at episode end. A wrong tool in the slot is
  FAIL.
- cable_clip ("Push the Displayport cable into the cable holder on the
  white block"): the DisplayPort cable is pushed into the cable holder
  on the white block and retained by the holder — the cable stays in
  the clip after the gripper releases — at episode end. Judge whether
  the cable is visibly seated/held in the clip in the final frames,
  not merely touching or lying next to the holder.
