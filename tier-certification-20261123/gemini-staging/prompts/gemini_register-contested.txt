# Judging brief — episode certification (blind judging)

You are judging whether recorded human-teleoperated demonstration
episodes actually completed their task. Each episode has ONE contact
sheet PNG: two camera rows, six frames each, left→right in time
(0%, 35%, 60%, 80%, 92%, ~100% of the episode). Judge the END STATE —
what the final frames show — not how elegant the motion was.

## Verdicts (exactly one per episode)

- PASS — the final frames clearly show the task's completion
  end-state (definitions below), with the gripper no longer holding
  the target object.
- FAIL — the final frames clearly show the end-state is NOT the
  completion state. A success achieved mid-episode but undone by the
  end is FAIL — end state only.
- INDETERMINATE — the final frames do not let you determine the end
  state (object occluded in both views at the end, ambiguous, or
  unreadable frames). Use sparingly; if one camera shows it clearly,
  judge from that.

## Task criteria (each episode names its own)

- ep859 — counting task, "Adjust the bowl so that it contains two
  objects.": PASS = exactly two objects are inside the bowl at
  episode end, gripper released. FAIL = the bowl clearly contains a
  different number of objects at end.
- ep573 — block_stack, "Stack the colouful blocks on top of each
  other": PASS = every block in the scene is part of one vertical
  stack — each block resting on another block or on the base block —
  gripper released, stack standing at episode end. FAIL = any block
  not part of the stack, stack fallen, or gripper still holding a
  block at end.

## Output format (your final report: one line per episode, nothing else)

ep<index> | PASS|FAIL|INDETERMINATE | <one short clause: what the final frames show>
