# zk0 Roadmap — public summary

The public roadmap is published as a high-level summary page:

**[zk0 Roadmap — public summary](https://zk0.bot/docs/ROADMAP.html)** (source: `docs/ROADMAP.html` in this repo)

The detailed roadmap — mechanism design, audit findings, and week-by-week milestones — is maintained in the project's private systems of record and shared with network members. This follows zk0's two-tier publishing policy: public pages carry the direction and the claims we can stand behind today (replicated benchmark evaluation; auditable records), while implementation detail stays private until sharing it is valuable to the network.
